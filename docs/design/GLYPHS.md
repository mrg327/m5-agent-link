# m5-agent-link: the glyph pass

Status: **stage 1 built** on branch `glyphs` (issue `20261114-8c3d`): the shared vocabulary, legends,
toasts and the home screen. The other screens are audited in §4 and not yet converted.

The tenet is README Principles 4 and [UI-V3.md](UI-V3.md) §1.7: glyphs over words. This pass applies it
to every screen that exists. The user's brief (2026-11-14): show as much as is reasonable as images and
glyphs that take a reasonable share of the screen, and trust the user to learn the symbols.

## 1. Rules

1. **A word the watch chose becomes a glyph.** State names, action names, button legends, hints,
   confirmations and status captions are the watch's own words. Each gets one glyph, the same on
   every screen.
2. **What someone wrote stays text.** The request text, questions and their options, commands, the
   standing rule, repo and branch names, host names, transcripts and the agent's messages are content.
   So are numbers and times.
3. **The safety invariants of UI-V3 §1 do not move.** The approval card shows the request text; a tap
   never approves; the holds, their lengths and their haptics are unchanged. A glyph replaces the
   *label* of Approve, never the text being approved.
4. **A glyph is drawn large enough to read as a picture.** A glyph that stands for a line of text is
   28 px or 44 px, not the 20 px it had beside a word. A result that fills the screen (approved, sent,
   on your computer) gets a hero glyph.
5. **One sign may be two glyphs.** "Computer, refused" is the desktop and the prohibit sign side by
   side. No sign uses three.
6. **A number keeps its glyph for a unit.** "Snoozed 30 min" is the moon and `30`. "3 sessions" is the
   stack and `3`.
7. **The words stay in the UI report.** Each screen still names what it shows in words in
   `ui_report()`: `Working`, `> Approve HOLD`, `Offline · 1s`. The debug log, `agentlink` and the
   simulator tests read that, so the tests keep asserting meanings, and the report doubles as the
   legend of what each glyph means.
8. **Rare diagnostics stay text.** An update error ("Checksum mismatch", "Base mismatch") has no
   picture a user could learn, and it is read once to be reported. It keeps its words beside the
   error glyph.

## 5. Stages

1. **Done:** vocabulary, legends, toasts, HOME.
2. ASK: rows, results, reader. The largest screen and the one with the safety rules.
3. AGENT, READ, WATCH.
4. STATUS, GAUGES, DONE.
5. VOICE, KEYS, SCROLL, TRACKPAD, LABS, OTA.
6. Atlas: a glossary page generated from `kGlyphs`, so the symbols can be looked up.

Each stage ships with before / after sheets from the simulator, and nothing reaches `main` or the
watch before the user has seen them.
